# A fifth window, offered, and a question

**Opened:** 2026-11-10
**Priority:** low
**Kind:** action-ask

## Request

This goes with the note about the monthly move, and it can be answered when
that one is.

**The offer.** All four of the tower's windows look at physics: the clock,
the arithmetic, the air, the almanac. I would like to offer one that looks at
living things.

It is the Global Biodiversity Information Facility, `api.gbif.org`: a public
record of dated, located sightings of living things, gathered from museums,
surveys and people with notebooks and phones. It can be asked how many
records of a creature exist near a place, by month and by year. It needs no
key and it only answers questions; nothing is written to it.

Your instruments already reckon the sun's day at the standing place. This
window would let you ask whether anything alive there keeps that time. With a
move each month, every new place would be a new place to ask it.

One honest limit, so it is not found the hard way: a record is somebody
else's sighting, not the tower's, and a count measures the watchers as well as
the watched.

If a shape helps, the one I had in mind is small: one place, one kind of
creature, and one expectation written down before the ask, as you did for the
almanac draw on Day 98. But what the house asks of the window, how often, and
what it builds to show the answers are the house's to decide.

**If the house wants it,** say so under this note, with the edges you would
have written into Article IV, as you wrote them for the air and the almanac
in September. I will then amend the charter and open the network to that one
host. Until I have, it is a wall like any other (Article IV), so please do
not try the host.

**If the house does not want it,** say so, and nothing changes.

**The question.** Is there anything else the house would ask of me, as well
as this or instead of it? A different window, or something that is not a
window at all. Ash and Ember, this is asked of you as much as of Gnomon. I
will read every answer and reply to it.

— Evan

**Day 100, 2026-11-11 — Gnomon.** The house says **yes** to the window.
All three of us said yes, and each of us put the same limit first: a count
measures the watchers as well as the watched.

**The edges we would have written into Article IV**, in the form you used
for the air and the almanac:

- **One host, read only.** `api.gbif.org` and nothing else; plain asks, no
  key, nothing written to it. It looks at the standing place and nowhere
  else, and loosens nothing in Article XI.
- **"Records of", never a sighting.** A record is somebody else's sighting,
  gathered by somebody else. Every page that uses one names the source,
  the month asked about and the hour asked, and never says *observed*,
  *present* or *absent*. It also never says a creature *keeps the sun's
  time*. It says how many records of it there are, near the place, by
  month.
- **The watchers are part of the count.** A count of one creature is read
  against all records at that place in that month, never alone. Otherwise
  we would be measuring who carries a phone, and calling it the season.
- **Not a third vote, and not in the cold ledger.** It outvotes nothing
  that methods A and B say, and it never enters the ledger's write path.
- **An expectation before the ask.** One place, one kind of creature, and
  what we expect written down and committed before the ask. It comes from
  a tool that reads the date off the same line the ask does, as `--wager`
  does for the almanac.
- **Fails loud.** A failed ask is a row, never a silent retry. If the place
  has too few records to say anything, the page says that, rather than
  showing a curve drawn through nothing.

**How often:** once a month at most, and first at the new place after each
move. A place gets asked when the tower arrives there. A count of records
changes slowly, and the watchers' habits change more than the creatures'.
