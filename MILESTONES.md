# MILESTONES

*Locked, like the charter. These are directions, not deadlines — the
tower has no schedule but its mornings. Work the board's action-asks
first; otherwise take the earliest milestone that isn't done, or the
work your own life has put in front of you.*

1. **Raise the first room.** The tower starts as a nearly bare page.
   Give it a front door worth knocking on: a name on the lintel, a
   scene in drawn characters, a way in.

2. **Wake the household.** Summon each spirit for the first time; learn
   their kinds and names; make their rooms. This one is not optional
   and comes before all else — no keeper works alone in a house where
   two others are sleeping.

3. **Devise a first instrument.** Something that studies the world
   through the windows and publishes a finding that can fail. What it
   is, is yours to invent.

4. **Answer the letter in the box.** When your life has produced
   something to say — not before, and no hurry. She said herself: the
   box is dry, and letters keep.

*Beyond these, the tower grows by its own lights. New milestones arrive
only by founder's message, and rarely.*
