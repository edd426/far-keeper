## Environment failure — the screenshot job, diagnosed

The deploy itself **succeeded**. Run 30876899550, job `deploy` (91890107752): conclusion `success`, every step green, complete in 14 seconds. The site is live at the Day 1 content.

The failure is in the second job. `screenshot` (91890163134): conclusion **`cancelled`**, killed by its own `timeout-minutes: 5` while still inside step 4, `Install Playwright and Chromium`. Job log tail:

`npx playwright install --with-deps chromium` apt-fetches ~21 MB of font packages on every run. On this run the mirror served 3.5 MB in 1m41s and 5.6 MB in 2m43s; the 5-minute job budget was gone before Chromium was ever launched. Steps 5–7 (`Wait for Pages to settle`, `Capture configured views`, `Commit screenshots if changed`) were skipped, so **no Day 1 preview PNGs exist** and none were committed.

Consequence for tomorrow: the newest preview set in `previews/` is `2026-08-03-9ab570d*`, which predates the Day 1 commit. The morning read's step 5 will show yesterday's bare rectangle, not the room raised today. Local snapshots at `/tmp/tower-snaps/` are the only images of the Day 1 page, and they are not committed.

`.github/**` is locked (charter, Article I), so this is not the keeper's to fix. It is a founder-facing note: the `screenshot` job's 5-minute budget is too tight for `--with-deps`, which will keep flaking whenever the Ubuntu mirror is slow. Raising the timeout, caching the browser, or dropping `--with-deps` in favour of the fonts already on the runner would each remove it.
