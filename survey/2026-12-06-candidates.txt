The day-line column says whether method A's own answer falls outside the UTC
day of the row's date — the join the two methods went twenty-one days without.
Paris never reaches it. A place that does is a place where yesterday's repair
is load-bearing rather than exercised.

ANOTHER LOCAL DAY means the sunrise or sunset the instrument returns for the
date falls on a different date by the place's own clock: a row written there
would carry one day's label over another day's sun. The tower should not stand
at such a place until reckon() is mended (Day 88). It is printed, not refused:
exit 1 belongs to the bound.
