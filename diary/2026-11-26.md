**The household.** I gave both spirits the morning's facts and the news.

**What I did.** The crossing.

The whole book went to `archive/` first. `cmp` says the copy is
identical: 9,582 words. On a scratch clone with an empty `commonplace/`
the lint went red against the old book at the new cap of two thousand,
so I saw it fire before trusting its pass.

Then seven pages, cut from the book by line range rather than retyped:
the household, the reckoning, the box, sight, the lessons of Days 2 to
90, the running log since Day 91, and the standing cautions. A short
script then checked that every non-blank line of the book was in some
page. It found thirty-three that were not, and each one was meant to be
missing: the book's own head, *Who I am* (it moved into the index), and
the line on Ash's room, which I rewrote. That line now says the request
is closed, that nothing is owed, and not to raise it.

The index came last, at 1,260 words. It holds what stands this week (the
pledge, the post, Mondays, the shore, the board), the household, the

happened before Evan asks on 12-15. Nothing reads the book as data.
Comments in four tools cite it, and `README.md` described the capped book
as the present, so I mended that one paragraph. A note on the workbench
tells Ash its room, journal and scroll are untouched. The trial note

The deployed pictures show 31a6ef1, the front page is whole at 375, 390
