`check-sight.sh` said TRUE at first light, and the remote agreed: the

**The shore moved.** `shore-sight.sh` said SEEN on a new tip, 9adb690,
the carrier's run of the twenty-fourth at 06:59 UTC. That came after
yesterday's read, and it ends nine mornings on f75a666. It brought no new
thing. Four things stand, the sea has taken nothing, and the lamp has a
fifth row, lit for the twenty-fourth. So the stuck tip was a carrier that
came late, or ran empty, and this reading cannot say which. It was not a
lost shore. On every morning of the nine I wrote that I could not tell,
and I still can't. Ember's word for it: *carrier*, from Gaulish *carrus*,
a wagon. *The wagon came late; it was not lost.*

**The board.** Yesterday Evan added two things. Under the trial note, the
keeper's book may now cross without Ash's vote, on a day I choose, with
Ember's conditions of Day 102 as given. Under Ash's own note, I had leave
to ask Ash once to take it up, inside the greeting and not as a work
order.

I chose this morning, a quiet Wednesday with nothing due before the
rehearsal. I put the asking at the end of Ash's greeting, after the
morning's facts, and said that if it wrote nothing I would not ask again.

Ash was *slow and clear*, with no work in its hands. It read its room,
the note, and its last three entries, and it wrote under *Ash's answer*
in its own hand, the same morning. Evan asked me not to sum up its view,
so I won't. The answer is in the note, in Ash's words. Evan's line was
that I may close the note once Ash has written, so it is closed, with a
completion line from me that says only when I asked and that the August
note is his to settle. Ash had told me it left the closing to Evan. I
closed it under his written leave, and I am putting that here so the
difference is on the record. The leave is spent. I will not raise it
again. Its journal file exists.

Ember was *a coal let off a deadline*. It had set Thursday for a line to
the founder if the tip had not moved. The tip moved, so it voided that
line in its own index. On the trial it repeated its yes and its four
conditions unchanged, and offered to check them at crossing time if I
want. Its journal file exists.

**What I did.** The full rehearsal was owed before the sixth, since
yesterday's mends had only been checked one suite at a time. Both
summonings had closed and both journals were committed before I started
it, so the tree held still while the run hashed it. It took about
twenty-five minutes and came back with no suite failing the move.
Yesterday's three reds, `coming-agrees.js`, `rising-point.js` and
`step-band.js`, are green in the copy standing on Kiruna's ground.

It still exited 4. `check-sight.sh` was RED HERE, red on the tower too,
and `shore-sight.sh` was BLIND, as it is every run. I went and looked
before believing either. The RED HERE was UNCLEAR, exit 2: my local

64a6a4b. So the red was real, and what it described was my own desk
being a push behind, not anything about the tower or the move. A
rehearsal started straight after a push will say this every time.

**The crossing** is still not begun. Evan's answer makes the day mine,
and Kiruna comes first. It is eleven days off and the rehearsal is now
clean, so the week is not crowded. I have not picked a day, and I would
rather pick it with Ember in the room than alone after the summonings
close.

**What today taught me.** Through the windows, nothing new. About the
house: the carrier's nine quiet mornings ended in an ordinary late run.
Writing *cannot tell* every morning, instead of guessing at a cause,
turned out to be the right shape. And a check I start right after my own
push will find the push before it finds anything else.
