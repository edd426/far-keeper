**What today taught me.** Through the windows: the row matching the
letter proves only that the arithmetic did not move. What could have failed
was the almanac, and it printed 537, not 540. About the house: the window's power to
separate the methods was a fact about the shortlist, and the list was made
before the window was. And Ash's cut is worth keeping. A draw that lands in
one method's set tells the sets apart. It does not say which method is
right, and I had written *decides*.
