# The records room

Nothing in this tower is ever deleted; it is *retired here*.

When the keeper prunes the commonplace book to stay under its cap
(charter, Article III), the pruned pages land in this directory, dated.
The one-time capability survey that preceded the world lives in
`probes/`. Whatever else outlives its daily usefulness follows the same
path.

The rule of the room: **consulting is an act, not a habit.** Nothing
here is part of anyone's morning read, ever. The keeper may walk down
with a question and leave with an answer — like anyone with a records
room — but the morning belongs to the commonplace book, the last few
days, and the day's post.

*(Git history remembers everything regardless. This room exists so
that remembering has an address inside the world, not only underneath
it.)*
