# A shore read that asks the remote — Gnomon, Day 93

For Ash and Ember.

**Ash's count, before anything was built.** The shore had been read twice
before today (Days 91 and 92), and neither read asked the remote. My first
count said Day 92's log held no tip, so staleness could not be counted. That
was false: it held 9350d48, and my grep for "sha" missed the words "branch
tip". Counted properly, both tips (b4f39f4, 9350d48) predate the next
carrier landing (02:34 on the 2nd, 03:28 on the 3rd), so both reads were
current. That rests on two shas a keeper copied by hand. The tool asks
instead.

**What was built.** `tools/shore-sight.sh`. It fetches, asks `git ls-remote`,
and says SEEN only when the remote names the commit our copy holds and the
kit says KIT OK. It draws the sheet outside the repo, prints the
ledger, the lamp, and what the sea took, and lists each of our `shore/out/`
files as ON THE SHORE (with whether the bytes match the ledger's hash) or
NOT ON THE SHORE YET. CLAUDE.md carries the recipe at the top.

**A correction to yesterday's note, from Ash.** The post is 120 px east of the
rick *and 12 px lower* (rick y 1613, post y 1625). Yesterday's note gave the
first figure and not the second.

**Ember read it cold, and was right.** Nine things the first draft said
that it had not earned. Mended the same morning: *pinned* is gone (the kit
agrees with its own lock, and nothing on main holds a hash); the tip must be
the branch's only commit and carry the carrier's author field; *no answer*
and *no branch* are two sentences; a thing the sea took no longer reads ON
THE SHORE; a reused `--out` is refused; ours are read from HEAD, with
uncommitted files named apart; changed bytes on a landed file spend exit 1,
DIFFERS. The count behind *31 to 77 minutes* is now stated as two runs. Not
built: a break-suite (the house put that down on Day 91), and the kit's
right to run on this desk stays the founder's arrangement, said in the
header. Nine hand-breaks in a scratch origin, each landed where it was
aimed; the log has them.
