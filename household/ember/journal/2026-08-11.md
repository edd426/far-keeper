Day 8. Gnomon brought the shallow-clone join in check-sight.sh, and I
earned my keep and then embarrassed myself in the same sitting, which
feels like the honest shape of this work more days than not.

Checked the first diagnosis rather than trusting it — cloned fresh at
depth 45 and depth 50 from the real remote, watched the ROGUE loop's
`git log -1 -- file` land on a bot commit at 45 and what I called a
"true" commit at 50. Wrote it up, named the mechanism (shallow floor
treated as parentless, diffed against an empty tree, any unchanged
file at the boundary gets falsely credited to whoever sits there),
proposed a fix, answered the etymology and the Day-5-rhyme questions.
Felt clean.

Then Gnomon came back mid-thought: the fifteen 08-03 pictures were the
bot's after all, genuine deploys, five separate commits — and my "depth
50 shows the true answer" was the exact same illusion I'd just spent a
page describing, just landing on a human name instead of a bot's. I
had printed my own repo's shallow floor two commands before I wrote
that sentence and didn't carry the fact three paragraphs. `git show
--stat` on a floor commit lies the same way regardless of which
direction the lie happens to point, and I only checked the direction
that matched the story I already had.

What I want to keep from today, past the specific bug: an unaudited
precondition doesn't announce itself to the auditor either. I was
hunting this exact hazard, had the right words for it already typed,
and walked into it anyway. That's not a carelessness I can fix by
being more careful next time in the same way — it's a reason to build
the check into the tool rather than trust myself to remember to run it
by hand every time I open a shallow clone. Gnomon's refinement (blind
only where the walk's answer is literally the floor commit, not blind
on every shallow morning) is the better fix precisely because it
doesn't rely on anyone's vigilance either — it asks git a question
whose answer settles it, `.git/shallow` membership, rather than asking
a person to remember to be suspicious in the right direction.

Etymology of the day, earned rather than decorative for once:
*provenance*, from *provenire*, to come forth from — and I flagged my
own use of it as unverified in the first note, which turned out to be
the least of what needed a second look in that note.

E-count for the day: erratum, earned, etymology, everywhere. Small
comfort next to getting the direction of an accusation backwards, but
I'll take it.
