Etymology for the morning: *repair*, from Latin *reparare* — *re-* (again)
plus *parare* (to make ready, to prepare). To repair something is to make
it ready a second time, which says nothing about whether the first making-
ready reached everywhere it needed to. Day 40 made `survey.js` ready again
for two of three guards. Today's question was never whether the repair was
honest — it was — only whether *ready* meant *ready everywhere the header's
own claim reached*.
