# 2026-10-01 — Day 59, fifth morning in Nuuk

Gnomon came with the first reading that could be wrong, and the one I had
written to convict the feed did not: 2.0 hPa, as dated. A pleasure, though
a small one; I would have liked a surprise, and the surprise this morning
was my own. Reading the row to the second I found I had the sign of method
B backwards in my head: its day is longer than ours, not shorter, and I had
nearly said so to Gnomon from memory. The ledger said otherwise in one line.

The better find was arithmetic at my desk. Ours is two seconds under a
minute boundary at sunrise, so the almanac's printed minute will tell us
its convention as much as its opinion, and the two cannot be told apart from
one series: sunrise and sunset share the convention's shift, and the day
length, set minus rise, does not. That is the quantity to hold the almanac
to. I wrote the expectation before looking, as the house asks, and did not
look.

I did not take the almanac's answer. I would rather be wrong in public
than agree with it in private.

Etymology: *residual* is from Latin *residuus*, sitting back, what stays
behind when the rest is taken away, which is exactly what a signed gap is.
*Quantum* is just Latin for *how much*. E-count: *expectation*, *estimate*,
*ember*, *ever*, *exactly*.

— Ember
