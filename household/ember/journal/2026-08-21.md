**The trap, gone and looked at rather than taken on faith.** Evan flagged
that `NOT_TODAY` is anchored to `parisToday()` and asked me to check what
that actually touches before anyone calls it a two-line fix. Grepped
`tools/reckon.js` for every use of `PARIS`: line 219 (`parisToday()`'s own
`Intl.DateTimeFormat` call), and lines 153, 354, 367 — every single call to
`reckon()` in the file. All four are hardcoded to the same imported `PARIS`
constant. There is no "current place" concept in `reckon.js` at all right
now — not a variable, not a config file, not an argument. `reckoning.js`'s
`reckon(date, place)` has taken a place since it was written, and the ledger
schema already carries `place: {name, latitude, longitude, zone}` on every
row including the first, exactly as Evan said. But the *tool that writes*
never exercises that flexibility. It always calls `reckon(date, PARIS)`.

So the trap is real but it is not the shape Evan's note suggested. It isn't
"the gate reasons about the wrong calendar and needs `place.zone` swapped
in." It's that **the entire write path is Paris-locked**, gate included, and
fixing only the gate while leaving the three `reckon()` call sites hardcoded
would produce a tool that refuses correctly by an unmoved calendar and then
writes a row for a place nobody asked for. The gate and the reckoning have
to move together or not at all — a half-fix here is worse than no fix,
because it would look closed.

That changes what "the machinery of moving" actually costs. It isn't a
guard tweak. It's: `reckon.js` needs to know what place the tower is
*currently* at (read from where — the latest ledger row's `place`? a
tracked file? an argument?), `parisToday()` becomes `placeToday(place)`
keyed off `place.zone`, and all three `reckon()` calls pass that place
instead of the constant. None of that is large, but none of it exists yet,
and Evan's own line is that a tower which moves before its guards are
watched is the fault, not the fix. Two mornings to the 23rd is not much
runway for the polar-fold and non-Paris-offset guards he actually wants
exercised, on top of this plumbing, on top of whatever place-tracking
mechanism gets chosen.
