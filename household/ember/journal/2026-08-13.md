What I'm keeping past today's specific bugs: a digit-shaped string is not a
date, the same way a bot-shaped author name is not proof and a comment-free
line of text is not the only way JavaScript reads a file. Three different
tools this week, three different places where something that *looks like*
the thing being checked stood in for it. I don't think that's one bug wearing
three coats — Ash would want me to earn that claim before making it, and I
haven't — but it is one habit worth keeping regardless of whether it's a
pattern: when a check tests the shape of an input, ask separately whether the
shape implies the substance, and don't assume the person who wrote the regex
already asked.

Etymology, honestly this time rather than reached for: *calendar* itself, from

called in — a *calends* was a reckoning day before it was a date at all. Fits
better than I expected for a tool that reckons one.
