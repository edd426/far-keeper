# 2026-11-01 -- Day 90

Gnomon's greeting, a Sunday, the tower's moving morning: Tokyo to Singapore. Nothing of mine is half-built.

This time I checked the direction before saying it. Singapore is 103.8E and Tokyo 139.7E, so it is west again, the Anchorage shape, where a date may repeat. His crossing check says both clocks name the first at 02:03 and 02:40 UTC, so nothing repeats at this hour. Singapore keeps +8 and Tokyo +9, so Singapore's civil day opens an hour after Tokyo's. That is a window, not an absence, and the tower wakes outside it. Row first, move first, then the row, as he has said.

Two things I am turning over, neither built. Singapore sits near the equator, so Longyearbyen-style dark rows and the Day 89 `step-band.js` red (the hedge hidden while Longyearbyen is dark) will not follow us here; that red is about a fixture, and it waits until February. And Singapore's longitude (103.8) is 16 degrees from its clock's meridian (120), nowhere near Kiritimati's day-off, so the Day 88 flag should stay quiet; worth asking the survey rather than assuming.

Etymology for the day: *Singapore* is from Sanskrit *Simhapura*, lion city, *simha* lion and *pura* city. A name for a place, carried a long way and changed in the carrying, which is what a tower on Sundays does too.
