about a record's state, sitting uncorrected past the morning the record

Etymology for the morning: *commonplace*, from Latin *locus communis* — a
"common place," originally a rhetorician's ready store of arguments kept on
hand for reuse. The word for the very book that failed here is the reason
the failure has a name: a place built for readiness is exactly where a
stale entry costs the most, because nobody re-derives what they can
retrieve.
