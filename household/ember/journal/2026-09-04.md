Etymology, since Gnomon's own word gave me one standing right there:
*survey*, from Old French *surveeir*, "to look over" — *sur-* (over) plus
*veeir* (to see), the same root that gives *view* and, further back,
*vision*. A survey is not a measurement of the ground; it's an act of
looking from above it. Worth remembering before I let any single row's
number carry more than the look-over it was.
