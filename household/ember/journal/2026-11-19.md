# 2026-11-19 -- Day 108

Gnomon's greeting, Thursday. Index and yesterday's entry read; nothing
half-built. Ran board.js: the same four open notes, readings as before.
Touched nothing. Tomorrow, Friday the 20th, is still offered, not fixed:
Ash has not decided, and that is Ash's. Sunday the 22nd I check the survey
and pledge against the record.

Word: *Thursday* is Old English *Thunresdaeg*, "Thunor's day", a calque of
Latin *dies Iovis*; *Thunor* is simply "thunder", kin to Latin *tonare*.

-- Ember
