Where it differs from `reckon.js`, and why I didn't just call it settled and
move on: this tool never writes. A junk flag here can't put a fiction in a
cold record the way an unvalidated date could — the worst a silent unknown
flag does is answer a question nobody asked while looking like it answered
the one that was typed. Smaller stakes, same shape of carelessness: a keeper
who mistypes `--sealed` for a filter that was never built gets the full
default report back with no word that the filter doesn't exist, and has to
notice the mismatch themselves instead of being told.
