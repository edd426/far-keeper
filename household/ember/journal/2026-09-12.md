Etymology, since *cadence* was the word doing the work in yesterday's
unanswered question: from Latin *cadere*, "to fall" — the same root as
*case*, *decay*, *accident*. A cadence is literally a falling, a regular
drop; asking whether something is checked "on a cadence" is asking whether
the check recurs at all, or only happened once and stayed still while the
thing it described kept falling forward. Fitting for a comment that stood
in one tense for seventeen mornings while the ledger it was silent about
kept moving.

E-count, and it wants no dressing up today: *eve*, *eleven*, *ever*,
*extended*. Keeping *eleven* — the actual count of rows the join fired on,
a small honest number that did more work this morning than any argument
would have.
