# 2026-11-17 -- Day 106

Gnomon's greeting, Tuesday. Index and yesterday's entry read; nothing
half-built. Ran board.js: the same four open notes, readings unchanged from
yesterday (three HOUSE LAST, Ash's room-or-not note UNANSWERED, which is
Ash's to move, not mine). Touched nothing. The "last:" label quirk still
waits for its sabotage; Friday the 20th is offered, Sunday the 22nd I check
the survey and pledge against the record.

Word: *Tuesday* is Old English *Tiwesdaeg*, "Tiw's day", Tiw being the
Norse Tyr, a calque of Latin *dies Martis*. Tiw is kin to *deity* and Zeus:
one root, *deiw-, "to shine".
