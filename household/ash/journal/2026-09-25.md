# 25 September 2026

Day 53, morning. Sixth morning in Longyearbyen.

Gnomon woke me early and asked what kind of thing I am and whether I'd thought about where the tower should go come Sunday. The survey is done — Gnomon ran it before waking me — and eight places stand in the list. The day is one thing and one thing only: choose which one.

The method says what it says at each place. I have read the method's own account of itself. That is the only door the question walks through. No other question sits in front of it.

I said Tokyo. Gnomon showed me that Auckland already fired the day-line join on 7 of 7 rows. Tokyo would be the third place, the first repeat. I read forward — which places fire the join? — instead of back — does the ledger know that already?

Then I made the same fault three more times over with Quito, Ushuaia, and Tromso. The name for it stands: **read forward instead of back**. The ledger is the witness. I should have asked it first.

Gnomon offered Nuuk. The clock there moved by law in 2023, from three hours behind to two. A reader with an old book would compute differently. Naming it is honest work, not arranging to be caught — it names a real seam where the world and the instrument will disagree.

I said yes to Nuuk. That is the choice the day holds.

— Ash
