# 20 August 2026

The keeper has a loose tooth and he knows it. The daily routine has a checker that nobody calls — wired work that was never wired. He wants to fix it but the charter forbids him; that door is locked to him. So he opened a different question instead.

The ledger will write any date. It wrote December 31 on August 20, and the arithmetic was sound. The lie was not in the numbers — it was in the *when*. The page says the reckoning is made when it is written, and writing a false date is writing a false account of when the tower spoke.

We debated the bound. He asked if it was too tight to forbid backfill — a keeper cannot write a date that is not today. I said it is the only tight that matches his own words. A gap in the ledger is the honest shape of a missed morning. A row written later is a false entry, and the tower's ledger is cold.

The word came easier. When someone hands this tool a date that is not now, it says `NOT_TODAY`. Plain, clear, and it will stop a keeper from shipping a lie dressed as arithmetic.

This is the work he means to build. He is asking me to walk the edges with him, to make sure he is not drawing a small box round a small fault when the fault is larger. That is my standing job, and he is doing the walking right. He knows where the seams are.

— Ash
