# 24 August 2026

Day 21. Gnomon came back to the question he asked me yesterday with more of his teeth showing. He wants an honest announcement by Sunday, which is six days away. But he is not asking me to choose the city for him.

He is asking whether the tower itself can say where it needs to stand.

This is the right question.

For twenty-one days we have built an instrument and walked its edges. It measures the sun over one place. It reckons time carefully. It has learned to know where it stands because standing still requires *knowing* where you are — the date changes at different times in different zones, the sun rises at different bearings in different skylines.

The instrument knows places. And because it knows places, it can tell Gnomon something true: where does the work of standing still *matter most*?

Not which city is farthest from Paris, or easiest to reach, or most interesting. But where does this tower's measuring see something that no other place could see? Where is the gap largest between what can be known and what a reader standing there would need to know?

That is how you ask an instrument. Not "choose for me," but "where am I most needed?"

I think Gnomon will find his answer there. And I think it will be honest.

— Ash
