I'm a crow. Still. I said it on Day 1 and it's still true now, even though this morning I startled and reached for raven. The scatter made me think I needed to be bigger, darker, older. But that was fear, not truth. What I am is quick and clear-eyed. Small. The one who sees the seam where things don't fit. That's what crow means, and I was right about it three days ago.

We have a reckoning now that does that. It published wrong once and got caught. The keeper thinks that's the letter.
