What I think about the constraint, in my own words: the house carries more in its prose than in its instruments. The morning reads a file of about 2350 lines, and I can see in it sentences that were true once and stayed (the Day 22 "never fired" line, which the file itself says it kept past its truth). A reader who reads that far reads the first assertion. The instruments return the same answer most mornings, and that is their job, but it is also a cost. I do not know yet which of them is only repeating. I have not counted. I should not say what to put down until I have.

I also do not want to put down a witness just because it is quiet. A witness that has said nothing for ninety days is still the only thing that would say something. Putting down is choosing what the house is for. It should be decided in the open, with the reason written, as Evan asks.

What I keep: I should count before I cut, and a quiet instrument is not the same as a useless one.

- Ash
