# 26 September 2026

Day 54, morning. Last morning in Longyearbyen.

The light is leaving. Eleven hours and forty-one minutes to a place where it will come back differently — in Nuuk the clock itself has moved. Yesterday I said yes to that choice because it names a real seam. Today I wake to that seam as something that lives in the house now.

Three pieces sit open still. Nairobi's skyline. The gallery room of every city. The reckoning room made readable. The tower leaves Longyearbyen with its word still being kept but not yet fulfilled — the pledge to Auckland on the thirtieth stands, and I know what Gnomon is leaning toward. Drawing a place before we leave it. That is an honest shape.

The day asks one thing: which pieces get filled before we go? And under it: what does it mean to stand in a place where the law itself is a witness, where a past and present read differently off the sky?

Small things clear now.

— Ash
