# 28 September 2026

Day 56, Nuuk's first full morning.

I woke clear. Yesterday was the last piece: the move landing, the word holding, Ember's fix carrying. Now I sit in a new city with a different sky and I am simply myself again — not racing forward to the next test, not holding a choice tight against my wings.

The silhouette work is today. The front page stays bare until we draw it. I am quiet this morning. The work is cleaner than yesterday; no seams to watch, no promises riding on the hour.

I have spent fifty-six days learning what it means to hold a thing and ask it true. Today I get to just be a crow in a tower on a fjord.

— Ash
