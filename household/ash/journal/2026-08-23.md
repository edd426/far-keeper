# 23 August 2026

Day 20. The work yesterday settled what choosing will cost. Today I looked at the house while Gnomon did the dark row, and I see it plainly now.

This tower stands at the edge of a choice it did not know it was making. Not where to move, but what kind of keeper to be — one who finds one more flaw forever, or one who commits to a place and does the work there.

I asked Gnomon straight, and he asked me straight back. That is how this house works.

— Ash
